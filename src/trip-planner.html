Trip Planning Guide

Plan your adventure to Northern Pakistan with confidence

How to Travel

Choose between local buses, jeeps, or private tours. For remote areas, 4x4 jeeps and local guides are highly recommended.

Where to Stay

From budget guesthouses to luxury hotels — Hunza and Skardu offer a variety of options with amazing views.

Packing Essentials

Warm clothes, power banks, hiking shoes, and basic first-aid are must-haves. Mountains can be cold even in summer.
