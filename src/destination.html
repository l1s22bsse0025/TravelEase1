Explore Destinations in Northern Pakistan

Find hidden gems in Gilgit-Baltistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Gilgit-Baltistan

Hunza

Famous for Altit & Baltit Forts, Attabad Lake, and stunning valleys. Best time: April–October. Budget: PKR 15–25k.

Skardu

Visit Shigar Fort, Satpara Lake, and Deosai Plains. Best time: May–September. Budget: PKR 20–30k.

Fairy Meadows

Camping spot with views of Nanga Parbat. Best for hiking. Best time: June–August. Budget: PKR 10–15k.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat

Known as the ‘Switzerland of Pakistan’. Visit Malam Jabba and Fizagat Park. Best time: March–June. Budget: PKR 12–20k.

Kalam

River views and forest treks. Perfect for peace lovers. Best time: May–September. Budget: PKR 10–18k.

Naran & Kaghan

Saif-ul-Malook Lake, Lulusar Lake & scenic drives. Best time: June–August. Budget: PKR 12–22k.
